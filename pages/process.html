The Journey from Start to Settlement

Here is exactly what to expect when you partner with us for your mortgage.

Step 1
chat

Discovery & Strategy Call

We begin with a brief 15-minute discussion to understand your requirements, financial goals, and timelines. We'll outline your borrowing capacity estimate and talk through potential mortgage structures that align with your plans.

Your Checklist: No documents needed yet. Just your goals, current salary details, and approximate deposit.
Step 2
account_balance

Product Comparison & Recommendation

Using our advanced lending software, we compare hundreds of home loan products from 65+ Australian banks, credit unions, and non-bank lenders. We compile a shortlist of options showing you comparison rates, upfront/ongoing fees, features (like offsets or redraws), and why they meet the Best Interests Duty.

Your Checklist: We'll ask you to securely upload payslips, bank statements, and ID documents to verify your profile.
Step 3
task

Application Submission

Once you select a loan option, we compile the application and package all supporting documentation to meet the chosen lender's strict criteria. We deal directly with the lender's assessors to resolve any questions, saving you hours of phone calls and follow-ups.

Step 4
thumb_up

Conditional Pre-Approval

The lender reviews the application and issues a conditional approval (or pre-approval). This establishes a clear purchase budget so you can bid at auctions or make offers on properties with confidence, knowing the bank is supportive of your finances.

Step 5
home_work

Valuation & Unconditional Approval

When you purchase a property, we submit the signed contract to the lender. The lender will often arrange an independent valuation of the property. Once satisfied with the property valuation and any remaining conditions, the lender issues **Unconditional (Formal) Approval**, followed by the mortgage contract.

Step 6
celebration

Settlement & Beyond

We coordinate with your solicitor/conveyancer and the lender to ensure files are signed and the settlement goes smoothly. Money transfers, title deeds exchange, and the keys are yours! We also conduct annual reviews to make sure your rate remains highly competitive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge anything for your services?

No. Our mortgage brokering service is entirely free to you. The banks and lenders pay us directly when your loan settles. This commission does not increase the interest rate or fees you pay.

How does Best Interests Duty benefit me?

Best Interests Duty (BID) is a statutory obligation requiring licensed brokers to prioritize the customer's interest over their own. Unlike branch lending staff who can only pitch their own bank's products, we are legally required to find the absolute best fit for you across our entire panel.

How long does the loan process take?

Pre-approval typically takes between 2 to 7 business days, depending on the lender's turnaround times and the complexity of your situation. From formal contract signing to settlement, the process usually takes 30 to 42 days.
